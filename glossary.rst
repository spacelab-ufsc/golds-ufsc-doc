    ************
    Nomenclature
    ************

.. glossary::
   :sorted:

   ACS
      Attitude Control System.

   AEB
      *Agência Espacial Brasileira*, or Brazilian Space Agency.

   CDS
      CubeSat Design Specification.

   CI
      Continuous Integration.

   COTS
      Commercial Off-The-Shelf.

   DoD
      Depth of Discharge.

   EDC
      Environmental Data Collection.

   EMMN
      *Estação Multimissão de Natal*, or Natal Multi-Mission Station in english.

   EPS
      Electrical Power System.

   FEC
      Forward Error Correction.

   FPGA
      Field-Programmable Gate Array.

   GMAT
      General Mission Analysis Tool.

   GOLDS
      Global Open Collecting Data System.

   HIL
      Hardware-In-the-Loop.

   HMAC
      Hash-based Message Authentication Code.

   INPE
      *Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais*, or National Institute for Space Research.

   MPPT
      Maximum Power Point Tracking.

   OBDH
      On-Board Data Handling.

   PCB
      Printed Circuit Board.

   PCD
      “Plataforma de Coleta de Dados”, or Data Collection Platform.

   SBCD
      *Sistema Brasileiro de Coleta de Dados*, or Brazilian Data Collection System.

   SDR
      Software Defined Radio.

   SHA-1
      Secure Hash Algorithm 1.

   SINDA
      *Sistema Nacional de Dados Ambientais*, or National System of Environmental Data.

   SNR
      Signal To Noise Ratio.

   TLE
      Two-Line Element.

   TTC
      Telemetry, Tracking and Command Module.

   WBS
      Work Breakdown Structure.

   WP
      Work Package.
